My Story: The Nonlinear Path to People Leadership

When I look back at my journey, it feels more like a roller coaster than a straight path—filled with unexpected turns, exhilarating highs, and moments of pure, high-octane excitement. If you had asked a younger version of me what I wanted to be, the answer would have been a scientist, astronaut, or doctor. Building HR ecosystems for global capability centers and hyper-growth enterprises? Not even on my radar.

The Multitasking Superpower: Born and brought up as a proud Delhiite, my primary native language was Hindi, but I was stubbornly determined to master English. My methodology? Total sensory immersion. I devoured books, analyzed movies, and meticulously imitated pronunciations. I became a pro at simultaneously listening, watching, analyzing context, and interpreting subtitles on the fly. Looking back, that was my earliest crash course in a vital human leadership trait: multi-layered situational processing.

From the Lab to the Boardroom: My entry into HR was wonderfully serendipitous. After an intensive sprint toward medical entrance exams, I pivoted into Biochemistry. By my third year, however, I realized that isolated laboratory benches couldn’t contain my energy—I needed human ecosystems. I leaped into business architecture and secured my MBA.

In 2017, during an internship at Nine dot Nine Mediaworx, something clicked. I realized I had an innate knack for decoding human capital and structural talent mapping. Shortly after graduating, I took a trial-by-fire role at Zashed Fashiontech. As the standalone HR pillar, I built their entire human operations module from scratch, expanding the engineering and operational footprint from 7 to 30 people in just two months. That trial earned me my first corporate honor: the "Calm under Pressure" award.

The Evolution into a Strategic Manager: My trajectory has always been shaped by big challenges. At OSIM India, sudden structural turnover left me managing the complete corporate HR department completely solo within two months of joining. I handled everything—from core talent acquisition and regulatory compliance to employee engagement. When the pandemic hit, because we operated under essential medical services, I coordinated field safety, managed remote transitions, and kept personnel spirits high, earning a "Covid Warrior" distinction.

Making My Space in Tech: After establishing a powerful cultural track record in Delhi, I made a conscious, strategic move to **Bengaluru** to cement my footprint inside high-growth modern ecosystems. Today, with over 8 years of foundational people leadership experience, I align business objectives with real human development. I am an ambivert who balances analytical focus with human connection. When I am recharging, you can find me reading deeply, keeping pace with K-Dramas, enjoying live music concerts, attending standup comedy, or traveling.
